Your Five Tasks

Task 01
Phase 1 — Break the Ice

Human Bingo

⏱ 15–20 minutes 👥 Whole group — move around the room

What you'll do

Each person receives a Bingo card with squares like "Has built something with their hands," "Speaks more than two languages," "Has lived outside Nigeria," "Can name three AI tools." Move around the room, talk to people, and find someone who matches each square. Write their name in it.

Goal

  • Fill as many squares as possible before time is called.
  • The person who fills the most squares wins — but the real prize is the conversations you just had.

Rules

  • You cannot use the same person for more than two squares.
  • You must actually speak to each person — no writing names from across the room.
  • Be honest with your answers.

Note: No presentation required for this task. It is an icebreaker — just move, talk, and meet your team.

Task 02
Phase 2 — Learn How People Think

The Survival Challenge

⏱ 20–30 minutes 👥 Small groups of 4–6
📍 Scenario: Your team's plane has crashed in the Sahara Desert. You are 100km from the nearest settlement. It is midday. The temperature is 40°C. You must survive and be found.

What you have — rank these items (1 = most important)

Signal mirror
5 litres of water
Rope (10 metres)
Fuel canister
Blanket
Compass
Broken radio
3 days of food
First aid kit
Sunglasses (x6)
Knife
Tarpaulin

Process

  1. Each person ranks the items individually (5 minutes).
  2. As a group, discuss and agree on a final ranking (15 minutes). Everyone must contribute. No one can stay quiet.
  3. Share your group ranking with the room and explain your top 3 choices.

Watch who emerges as...

👑 The Leader
🧩 The Analyst
🕊️ The Negotiator
💡 The Creative
🔇 The Quiet Thinker

No presentation required. Debrief with your group: Who led? Who listened? Who changed their mind and why?

Task 03
Phase 3 — Build Together

Tallest Tower Challenge

⏱ 30 minutes 👥 Small groups of 4–6 🏆 Winner announced

What you'll need

A4 paper (20 sheets)
Masking tape (1 roll)
Straws (optional)
Scissors (1 pair)

Rules

  • Build the tallest free-standing tower you can in 25 minutes.
  • "Free-standing" means it must hold itself up for at least 10 seconds without anyone touching it.
  • You may not attach the tower to the table, wall, or any fixed object.
  • Use only the materials provided.
  • All group members must physically contribute to building.

Scoring

  1. Height (measured from table surface to highest point) — 60 points
  2. Stability (held for 30+ seconds) — 20 points
  3. Creativity of design — 20 points

After building, each group gets 60 seconds to explain their design strategy to the room.

Task 04
Phase 4 — Dream Together

Design the Ideal African City in 2050

⏱ 30–45 minutes (prep) + 3-min presentation 👥 Small groups of 4–6 📊 PowerPoint required

Your brief

It is 2050. Your group has been commissioned to design the flagship African city of the future — a city that proves to the world what Africa can build when it leads with imagination and technology. This city can be anywhere in Africa. You name it. You design it.

Your city must address at least 4 of these themes

🚆 Transportation
⚡ Energy
🔐 Security
🎓 Education
🤖 AI & Robotics
🏥 Healthcare
🌾 Agriculture
🏗️ Housing
💧 Water
📡 Connectivity

Process

  1. Name your city and give it a founding vision statement (1 sentence).
  2. Agree on at least 4 themes your city will solve for.
  3. For each theme, describe one specific technology or system your city will use.
  4. Sketch your city layout (rough is fine — on paper or whiteboard).
  5. Prepare a 3-minute PowerPoint presentation to present to everyone at RAIN.
📊
Presentation required — PowerPoint, 3 minutes per group Your group will present to the full RAIN team. Your slides should include: your city name and vision, a sketch or visual of the city layout, and your key technology choices per theme. Be bold. Be specific. Think like builders, not dreamers.
Task 05
Phase 5 — Solve a Real Problem

AI & Robotics Solutions for Insecurity in Nigeria

⏱ 40–60 minutes (prep) + 5-min presentation 👥 Small groups of 4–6 📊 PowerPoint required
⚡ RAIN Focus Task

Context

Insecurity — banditry, kidnapping, farmer-herder conflict, urban crime, border threats — remains one of Nigeria's most urgent and costly challenges. Your group has been asked to design an AI-based or robotics-based solution that could meaningfully address one specific aspect of this problem.

🎯 This is not theoretical. RAIN builds real systems. Think like engineers, not students. What would you actually deploy?

Choose one focus area

Border surveillance
Urban crime detection
Forest/rural monitoring
Emergency response
School/campus safety
Conflict early warning

Your solution must address

  1. What is the specific problem? State it precisely. Use data or examples if you know them.
  2. What is your solution? Name the technology: drone, camera network, satellite, ML model, robot patrol, NLP alert system, etc.
  3. How does it work? Describe the system in plain terms — input, processing, output, action.
  4. Who deploys it? Government? RAIN? A community? A partnership?
  5. What are the risks or limitations? Be honest. Every system has tradeoffs.
  6. What would success look like? How would you measure impact?
🎤
Full Presentation — PowerPoint, 5 minutes per group + Q&A This presentation goes to the full RAIN team and may be reviewed by RAIN leadership. Your slides should cover: the problem, your proposed solution, how it works, a simple diagram or system map, deployment plan, and success metrics. Treat this as a real pitch. You are presenting a real solution to a real Nigerian problem.

Bonus challenge

If your group has time, sketch a prototype hardware or software architecture diagram on paper or a slide. What sensors, models, or robots does your system use? How does data flow from field to decision?

Facilitator Note

Optional: The Lost at Sea Challenge

This activity can be run in place of the Survival Challenge (Task 2), or used with a different orientation batch. A ship is sinking. Your team has access to: a mirror, water, rope, fuel, a blanket, a compass, a radio, and food. You must agree on what to save — and in what order. The discussion reveals the same leadership and collaboration dynamics as the Survival Challenge, but with a different scenario flavor.

🚢 "You have 10 minutes to decide. The ship goes under in 11."
