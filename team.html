Your Five Tasks
Task01
Phase 1 — Break the Ice

Human Bingo

⏱ 15–20 minutes👥 Whole group — move around the room

What you'll do

Each person receives a printed Bingo card with squares like "Has built something with their hands," "Can name 3 AI tools," "Has pulled an all-nighter for a project." Move around the room, talk to people, and find someone who matches each square. Write their name in it and ask them to initial it.

Rules

  • You cannot use the same person for more than two squares.
  • You must actually speak to each person — no writing names from across the room.
  • First to complete a row, column, or diagonal shouts BINGO!

📌 Bingo cards are printed and handed to you by admin staff. No presentation required for this task.

Task02
Phase 2 — Learn How People Think

The Survival Challenge

⏱ 20–30 minutes👥 Groups of 4–6
📍 Your plane has crashed in the Sahara. You are 100km from the nearest settlement. It is midday. 40°C. You must survive and be found.

Items — rank from most to least important

Signal mirror
5 litres of water
Rope (10m)
Fuel canister
Blanket
Compass
Broken radio
3 days of food
First aid kit
Sunglasses (x6)
Knife
Tarpaulin

Watch who emerges as...

👑 The Leader
🧩 The Analyst
🕊️ The Negotiator
💡 The Creative
🔇 The Quiet Thinker

No presentation. Debrief with your group: Who led? Who listened? Who changed their mind and why?

Task03
Phase 3 — Build Together

Tallest Tower Challenge

⏱ 30 minutes👥 Groups of 4–6🏆 Winner announced

Materials provided

A4 paper (20 sheets)
Masking tape (1 roll)
Straws (optional)
Scissors (1 pair)

Rules

  • Build the tallest free-standing tower in 25 minutes.
  • Must hold itself up for at least 10 seconds without anyone touching it.
  • Cannot be attached to the table, wall, or any fixed object.
  • All group members must physically contribute.

Scoring

  1. Height (table to highest point) — 60 points
  2. Stability (holds 30+ seconds) — 20 points
  3. Creativity of design — 20 points

Each group gets 60 seconds to explain their design strategy to the room.

It is 2050. Your group has been commissioned to design the flagship African city of the future — a city that proves to the world what Africa can build when it leads with imagination and technology. Name it. Design it.

Your city must address at least 4 of these themes

🚆 Transport
⚡ Energy

Task05
Phase 5 — Solve a Real Problem

AI & Robotics Solution to Insecurity in Nigeria

⏱ 40–60 min prep + 5-min presentation + Q&A👥 Groups of 4–6📊 PowerPoint required
⚡ RAIN Focus Task
🎯 This is not theoretical. RAIN builds real systems. Think like engineers, not students. What would you actually deploy?

Choose one focus area

Border surveillance
Urban crime detection
Forest/rural monitoring
Emergency response
School/campus safety
Conflict early warning

Your solution must address

  1. What is the specific problem? State it precisely with data or examples.
  2. What is your solution? Name the technology: drone, camera network, ML model, robot patrol, NLP alert system.
  3. How does it work? Input → processing → output → action.
  4. Who deploys it? Government? RAIN? A community partnership?
  5. What are the risks? Be honest. Every system has tradeoffs.
  6. What does success look like? How do you measure impact?
🎤
Full Presentation — PowerPoint, 5 minutes + Q&A to the full RAIN team and leadershipCover: the problem, solution, how it works, a system diagram, deployment plan, and success metrics. Treat this as a real pitch.
Facilitator Note

Optional: The Lost at Sea Challenge

Can replace the Survival Challenge (Task 2) with a different orientation batch. A ship is sinking. Your team has: a mirror, water, rope, fuel, a blanket, a compass, a radio, and food. Agree on what to save — and in what order. Creates the same leadership dynamics with a different scenario flavor.

🚢 "You have 10 minutes to decide. The ship goes under in 11."
