Kp: immediate response to error. Too high → oscillation, instability.
Ki: removes steady-state error over time. Too high → windup, slow response.
Kd: anticipates future error via derivative. Damps oscillations. Sensitive to noise.

Tuning order: Kp first → Kd to damp → Ki to remove offset.
C(s) = Kp + Ki/s + Kd·s
G(s) = Plant/(1 + Plant·C(s))
Closed-loop T(s) = C·P/(1+C·P)
